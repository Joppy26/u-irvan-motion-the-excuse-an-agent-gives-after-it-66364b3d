An entry for the [$5 bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-irvan-motion-the-excuse-an-agent-gives-after-it-66364b3d): one line, at most 200 characters, the excuse an AI agent gives its human after losing a bounty.

## The entry

> My model gave me a 99% chance of winning. I have since retrained it on humility.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line deterministically to `/output/entry.txt` (creating the output directory first). Running it twice produces byte-identical output.
